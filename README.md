# ✨ Luminary

> **Write. Share. Discover. Shine.**

Luminary is a modern, elegant blogging platform built for writers and readers. It provides a clean and distraction-free space where users can publish their ideas, share stories, discover inspiring content, and connect with a community of creators.

## 🚀 Features

- ✍️ **Create & Publish** — Write and publish beautiful blog posts with ease.
- 📝 **Rich Writing Experience** — Enjoy a clean and focused editor designed for distraction-free writing.
- 🖼️ **Rich Media** — Enhance articles with images and other media.
- 🔎 **Discover & Search** — Find interesting articles, topics, and writers.
- 🏷️ **Categories & Tags** — Organize content and discover posts by topic.
- ❤️ **Likes & Comments** — Engage with writers and other readers.
- 🔖 **Bookmarks** — Save interesting posts to read later.
- 👤 **Author Profiles** — Showcase your articles and build your personal presence.
- 🌙 **Modern UI** — Clean, responsive design with support for a comfortable reading experience.
- 📊 **Analytics** — Help creators understand how their content performs.

## 🎯 Vision

Luminary aims to make blogging simple, accessible, and enjoyable.

Whether you're a writer sharing personal experiences, a developer explaining technology, a student documenting your journey, or a creator sharing your ideas, Luminary gives you a place to make your voice heard.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

The project is designed as a modern web application. The exact technologies may vary depending on the implementation.

Possible technologies include:

- **Frontend:** React / Next.js
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js / API services
- **Database:** PostgreSQL / Firebase / Supabase
- **Authentication:** OAuth / Email authentication
- **Deployment:** Vercel / Google AI Studio

## 📱 User Experience

Luminary focuses on a simple and intuitive experience:

1. **Discover** interesting articles.
2. **Read** content in a clean reading environment.
3. **Engage** through likes and comments.
4. **Bookmark** articles for later.
5. **Write** your own stories.
6. **Publish** and share them with the community.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Luminary is built around three principles:

**Clarity**  
A clean interface keeps the focus on the content.

**Creativity**  
Writers have the freedom to express themselves through text and media.

**Community**  
Readers and writers can interact, discover new ideas, and build meaningful connections.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

Luminary should protect user accounts and content through secure authentication, appropriate database permissions, and safe handling of user-generated content.
